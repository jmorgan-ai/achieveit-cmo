# Social creative rules

Settled in Cristina's image workflow (export reviewed 2026-11-18). Applies to every draft's copy and image idea.

- No call to action on the image. The link goes in the post or the first comment (suggest the first comment: one line plus the primary page).
- Don't repeat copy: the headline and subhead must each add something. No em dashes anywhere.
- Formats that have been used: quote or stat graphic, annotated diagram, side-by-side comparison, short GIF, client spotlight (real dashboard
  screenshot plus a real customer quote), Dashboard of the Month, webinar promo (speaker photos), link post to a blog page.
- GIFs play once and stop on the last frame (LinkedIn may loop them anyway).
- Brand: use the real logo file, never retype it; Poppins at Semibold or lighter; blue headers (#006697), gray body (#666666), green accent
  (#1caf7b); orange (#f6b267) only on demo buttons; flat thin-border cards, no heavy drop shadows; organic blobs that bleed off the edge and the squiggle accent.
- Avoid the "AI-made" look: generic icons, uniform rounded cards, invented product UI. Use real screenshots and real quotes.
- Name only public customers; no hosts or customers someone has asked to leave out (check the latest Slack or meeting notes before drafting).
- Open gaps: no vector logo, no icon library, stock-photo licence for social unconfirmed. Don't use stock photos in drafts until confirmed.
